Know Your Food

Ingredients and descriptions of popular dishes

🍚 Idli – Ingredients & Description

Idli is a soft steamed South Indian dish prepared using fermented rice and urad dal batter. It is commonly served with sambar and coconut chutney.

🥞 Masala Dosa – Ingredients & Description

Masala dosa is prepared using fermented rice and urad dal batter and filled with seasoned potato masala, onions and spices.

🍗 Chicken Biriyani – Ingredients & Description

Chicken biriyani contains aromatic rice, chicken, onions, tomatoes, herbs and traditional Indian spices.

🫓 Parotta – Ingredients & Description

Parotta is a layered flatbread prepared using refined flour, oil and water. It is usually served with vegetable or non-vegetarian gravy.

🍮 Payasam – Ingredients & Description

Payasam is a traditional Indian dessert made using milk, jaggery or sugar, cardamom and nuts.
